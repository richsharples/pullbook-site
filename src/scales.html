Pulling a shot

No more shadowing the scale

Until now, pulling a shot meant working two devices at once — one eye on the scale, one thumb in the app, copying numbers across before the crema settled. This release removes the middleman: PullBook connects to your scale directly, and the numbers come to you.

What's new

  • Pair once, forget it. PullBook finds your scale over Bluetooth and reconnects automatically the next time you brew.
  • Live weight while you pull. The shot screen mirrors the scale in real time, so you can watch the yield climb without looking away.
  • Yields captured automatically. Your final weight lands on the shot record by itself — no typing, no rounding, no guessing.
  • The loop stays the same. Taste it, get one fix, pull again — just with fewer manual steps between shots.

Supported scales

We're starting with the Half Decent scale, with more to follow. Got a scale you'd like PullBook to talk to next? Tell us on the feedback page.
