What's new

Release notes for every PullBook beta, freshest first.

0.1.0-beta.16

BUILD 9 · 4 JULY 2026
  • Experimental Bluetooth scale support (for the Half Decent Scale) is now available for testers who turn on debug tools — see live shot weight during your pull, tare/zero manually, and try a new weigh-in screen for dialling in your dose before grinding. Note it stays hidden for everyone else for now.
  • You can now add a bag photo from your camera or photo library right from the bean edit screen, not just when scanning a new bag.
  • Fixed a startup hang where the app could get stuck on a blank brown screen and never load.
  • On iPad, the Home favourites shelf now wraps to show all your starred beans in landscape instead of cutting them off behind a sideways scroll.
  • Fixed unreadable stats (Total shots and Avg time) on the iPad bean profile in dark mode.

See the smart scale integration in action →

0.1.0-beta.15

BUILD 8 · 21 JUNE 2026
  • Returning users no longer get sent back through onboarding after an app update — the app now waits for your saved settings to load before deciding where to take you.
  • Added two new feedback buttons on the About page so you can report a bug or request a feature straight from a pre-filled email — no sign-in needed, and useful details about your device are attached automatically.
  • On the iPhone bean profile, the Pull button now sits directly below the recipe card so it's within reach without scrolling, and we've added extra space at the bottom so the floating tab bar no longer hides your content.
  • Fixed feedback emails so the attached diagnostics now display properly across mail apps like Gmail instead of being squashed onto one line.
  • Fixed an issue on iPad where bean profiles that inherit the machine's default brew temperature could fail to display correctly.

0.1.0-beta.14

BUILD 7 · 12 JUNE 2026
  • Brew temperature is now tied to your machine: in expert mode, switch on "Temperature control" on the machine card in Settings (for PID or dual boiler machines) and set a default temp. If your machine can't adjust brew temp, temperature is hidden everywhere to keep things tidy.
  • Recipe temperature now follows your machine's default automatically. You can pin a specific temp for a bean from the pull setup screen, and setting it back to the default un-pins it.
  • Old auto-filled roast-level temps that you never actually chose have been cleared out, so your settings reflect only the temps you've deliberately picked.
  • Every shot now records the exact temperature it was pulled at, and temperature suggestions only appear when your machine can actually act on them.
  • Removed the "That doesn't sound right" button on the shot review screen — it never did anything. We'll bring back something useful here later.

0.1.0-beta.13

BUILD 6 · 10 JUNE 2026
  • Taste and recommendation screens redesigned for iPad — a larger taste wheel and everything on one screen, no scrolling
  • The whole pull flow is now full-screen on iPad, matching the iPhone — no sidebar to tap mid-shot
  • The Pull button no longer scrolls away on the bean profile; bean info and stats stay on screen while the shot log scrolls
  • Dark mode fixes: the logo and the fix card now render correctly
  • Action buttons sit in the same place on every screen of the flow
